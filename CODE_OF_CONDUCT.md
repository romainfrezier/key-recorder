# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want Key Recorder to be a welcoming, respectful, and constructive project
for everyone. Participants are expected to:

- treat others with patience and respect;
- welcome different backgrounds, experiences, and points of view;
- give and receive feedback focused on the work, not the person;
- take responsibility for mistakes and help repair their impact.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, doxxing, and
other unacceptable behavior are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Please report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainers
through GitHub. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. A
maintainer may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation
when needed to protect the community.

This code of conduct applies in project spaces and whenever someone is
representing the project.
